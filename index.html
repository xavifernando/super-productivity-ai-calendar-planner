🤖 AI Provider
API keys and plugin settings are stored in this app's localStorage and are not isolated from other installed iframe plugins. Install only plugins you trust.
Built-in cloud providers use the app's host allowlist. Custom endpoints, Ollama, and calendar URLs use direct browser requests and are not constrained by the manifest host list.
When enabled, the finishDay hook reads configured calendars and task/project titles and estimates, then sends planning context to the selected AI provider.
🔗 Calendar Feeds
Feeds are fetched directly by the browser. The feed host receives the request and its URL, which may contain a private token.
🛠️ Schedule Maintenance & Cleanup Tools
🧹 Factory Reset Plugin Storage: Wipes all locally cached settings, API keys, custom habits, and saved drafts from your browser/app storage.
🗓️ Unschedule active date: Clears scheduling fields for incomplete tasks on Today or Tomorrow. Tasks, projects, and subtasks are preserved.
↩️ Undo Last Apply: Restores previous scheduling fields or unschedules newly created tasks; task content is preserved.
📖 Plugin Overview & How to Use
AI Calendar & Task Planner drafts a schedule from your calendar commitments and existing backlog. It can leave time open when there are fewer tasks than available slots and does not invent tasks to fill the day.
1
Connect Calendars: Add iCal subscription feeds in Calendar Feeds or upload an .ics file. Meetings are deduplicated and synchronized across timezones.
2
Habits & Rituals: Categorize your habits in Habits & Rituals. Use the category Toggle All switch or click 💾 Sync to Habits Section to register them into Super Productivity's native habit tracker (#/habits).
3
Projects Backlog: Use Projects Explorer to organize your tasks. Click ➕ Add to Today on any task to manually schedule it into today's timeline.
4
Dry Run: Review a draft using your existing tasks. Unfilled time remains open; applying an existing task updates its schedule instead of creating a duplicate.
5
Plan Ahead: Toggle between 📅 Today and ⏩ Tomorrow at any time to stage and schedule tomorrow in advance.
💡 Optional automation: Enable the finishDay checkbox in AI Providers to draft tomorrow automatically. This reads configured calendar feeds and task/project details and sends planning context to your selected provider.
