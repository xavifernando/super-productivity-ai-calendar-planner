📖 Plugin Overview & How to Use
AI Calendar & Task Planner is an automated daily executive assistant for Super Productivity. It reads your calendar commitments (Google Calendar, Outlook, iCal), computes exact free time gaps between meetings, and populates 100% of your open hours with project tasks and deep-work blocks.
1
Connect Calendars: Add iCal subscription feeds in Calendar Feeds or upload an .ics file. Meetings are deduplicated and synchronized across timezones.
2
Habits & Rituals: Categorize your habits in Habits & Rituals. Use the category Toggle All switch or click 💾 Sync to Habits Section to register them into Super Productivity's native habit tracker (#/habits).
3
Projects Backlog: Use Projects Explorer to organize your tasks. Click ➕ Add to Today on any task to manually schedule it into today's timeline.
4
Full Day Dry Run: Click 🔍 Dry Run (Full Day Plan) to let AI calculate continuous focus blocks for every open minute with zero dead time.
5
Plan Ahead: Toggle between 📅 Today and ⏩ Tomorrow at any time to stage and schedule tomorrow in advance.
💡 Pro Tip: When you finish your workday in Super Productivity, the finishDay hook automatically drafts tomorrow's schedule so it's ready when you wake up!
